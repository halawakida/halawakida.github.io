Our story

From my kitchen to your heart.

Shireen Abourehab has been making desserts for longer than she can remember. It has always been one of her favorite things to do, and for years everyone who tasted her baklava, kunafa and cakes told her the same thing: “These are so good, you should sell them.”

Now that her kids are off at college, she is finally doing something for herself. Every tray is still made the way she has always made them: by hand, in her own kitchen, with a lot of love.
